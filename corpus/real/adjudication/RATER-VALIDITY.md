# How accurate the raters are

Generated by `node scripts/rater-validity.mjs` from `rater-validity/`. Do not edit.

The ground truth for `corpus/real` comes from rater A (`claude-opus-5-5`), rater B (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) and, where they disagreed, a resolver (`claude-fable-5-1`). This page measures that process on cases whose answer is known: the same raters, under the same instructions and restrictions, rated every ACT test case of every rule Marlo implements, renamed so nothing revealed the expected outcome, and the disagreements went to the same resolver. The W3C's expected outcomes were written by people, so agreement with them is an accuracy figure for the raters.

594 cases. The raters agreed with each other on 96.5% of them (Cohen's kappa 0.947), and 21 went to the resolver.

A `cantTell` never counts as agreement, because no ACT expected outcome is `cantTell`. Failure precision and recall treat `failed` as the positive class, as the calibration table does for an engine.

**How to read this.** The adjudicated row is the error rate of the ground truth the real-page figures are graded against, measured on minimal test cases. Real pages are larger and messier, so it is a floor on that error, not an estimate of it. The test cases are public and may have been seen in training; the pages are public too.
